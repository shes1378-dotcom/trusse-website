About Trusse

Trusse is your AI contract manager for UK construction. It reads, drafts and manages your contracts, flags the clauses that cost money and reminds you before every notice is due.

Why Trusse Exists

Construction firms sign contracts every week, often without a commercial team on hand. Trusse is building a contract manager for every one of them, so they can sign with confidence and build with peace of mind.

Legal Expertise, Built In

General AI isn’t set up for UK construction contracts. Trusse is built for them: Trusse drafts it, an expert checks it, and you sign it. Every flag and answer points to the clause it comes from, so you can check it for yourself.
